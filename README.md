LearnJPD la nen tang hoc tieng Nhat truc quan, toan dien duoc xay dung theo chuan giao trinh Minna no Nihongo cua nha xuat ban 3A Corporation, ket hop kho du lieu Kanji chuyen sau tu N5 den N1. ## Tong Quan Du An

LearnJPD duoc thiet ke de dap ung day du lo trinh tu nguoi moi bat dau (N5) den trinh do cao cap (N1). He thong khong chi dung lai o viec tra cuu tu vung don thuan ma tap trung toi uu hoa qua trinh tiep thu qua:

- Ghi nho chu dong thong qua 8+ che do thuc hanh chuyen biet.
- He thong lap lai ngat quang (SRS) giup chuyen doi kien thuc vao bo nho dai han.
- Ho tro ngoai tuyen (Offline-first) ket hop tu dong dong bo khi co ket noi internet.
- Giao dien toi uu cho ca may tinh de ban va thiet bi di dong (PWA ready).

Noi dung tu vung va ngu phap duoc phan bo logic theo he thong sach Minna no Nihongo va cac chuyen de cao cap:

| Phan He | Trinh Do | So Luong Bai | Noi Dung Chinh |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Minna no Nihongo So Cap 1 | JLPT N5 | 25 bai (Bai 1 - 25) | Tu vung nen tang, chu Han co ban, vi du mau cau va hoi thoai Kaiwa |
| Minna no Nihongo So Cap 2 | JLPT N4 | 25 bai (Bai 26 - 50) | Hoan thien he thong ngu phap so cap, tu vung ung dung thuc te |
| Minna no Nihongo Trung Cap 1 | JLPT N3 | 12 bai (Chukyu 1) | Tu vung va cau truc trung cap phuc vu giao tiep tu nhien va on thi N3 |
| Minna no Nihongo Trung Cap 2 | JLPT N2 | 12 bai (Chukyu 2) | Tu vung chuyen sau, van phong hoc thuat va cong viec |

Moi muc tu vung deu bao gom:
- Chu Han (Kanji) va chu phien am (Hiragana / Katakana).
- Am Han Viet tieu chuan.
- Y nghia tieng Viet chi tiet.
- Mau cau vi du ngu phap va doan hoi thoai Kaiwa theo boi canh thuc te.
- Phat am mau (Native Audio TTS).

## He Sinh Thai Kanji Chuyen Sau

LearnJPD tich hop co so du lieu hon 2.100 chu Han voi lo trinh tiep can da chieu:

### 1. Phan Bo Cap Do JLPT
- JLPT N5: 79 chu can ban
- JLPT N4: 166 chu so trung cap
- JLPT N3: 367 chu trung cap
- JLPT N2: 367 chu trung cao cap
- JLPT N1: 1.232 chu cao cap

### 2. Thong Tin Chi Tiet Moi Chu Han
- Am Han Viet (in hoa).
- Am Onyomi va Kunyomi kem vi du ghep tu.
- So net viet va bo thu cau thanh (trong tong so 214 bo thu).
- Mo phong thu tu tung net viet (Stroke Order).
- Y nghia va boi canh su dung thuc te.

### 3. Cac Tinh Nang Kanji Dac Thu
- Kanji Roadmap: Lo trinh truc quan hoa tien trinh nam vung Han tu tu co ban den chuyen sau.
- Radicals Master: Bang tra cuu va thuc hanh 214 bo thu tieng Nhat kem y nghia va bien the.
- Look-Alike Kanji: Phan tich va so sanh cac chu Han de gay nham lan theo tung cap/nhom.
- Kanji Jukugo: Luyen tap va tra cuu cac tu ghep Han tu pho bien.
- Context Sentences: Ren luyen doc chu Han truc tiep trong cau van thuc te.

## Cac Che Do Hoc & Luyen Tap Thong Minh

Ung dung cung cap danh muc che do luyen tap phong phu nham phat trien dong deu 4 ky nang:

1. The Flashcard 3D
   - Giao dien lat the da chieu muot ma.
   - Mat truoc hien thi chu Han va phien am; mat sau cung cap Han Viet, y nghia, vi du ngu canh va am thanh phat am.
   - Tich hop danh gia muc do ghi nho truc tiep vao thuat toan SRS.

2. Che Do Nhoi Nhet (Cramming Mode)
   - Ho tro nhap lieu Romaji tu dong chuyen doi sang Hiragana theo thoi gian thuc qua bo go IME tich hop.
   - He thong goi y ky tu linh hoat va kiem tra do chinh xac tung ky tu.

3. Trac Nghiem (Practice Test & Quiz)
   - Kiem tra phan xa voi he thong cau hoi 4 dap an co gioi han thoi gian.
   - Cham diem tu dong, tong ket ket qua va phan tich cac cau sai ngay sau phien hoc.

4. Go Tieng Nhat (Japanese Typing)
   - Ren luyen toc do go va do chinh xac ban phim tieng Nhat.
   - Theo doi toc do WPM va ti le chinh xac theo tung bai hoc.

5. Luyen Chia Dong Tu (Conjugation Trainer)
   - Bang tong hop quy tac chia dong tu (Cheat Sheet).
   - Luyen tap chuyen doi cac the: The Te, The Qua khu, The Kha nang, The Menh lenh, The Bi dong, The Sai khien.

6. Nghe Duoi (Shadowing Mode)
   - Luyen phat am va ngu dieu chuan ban xu.
   - Tuy chinh toc do phat (0.8x, 1.0x, 1.2x), so lan lap lai, khoang nghi va che do nghe khong phu de (Blind Listening).

7. Ghep Tu (Match Game)
   - Tro choi tuong tac ghep cap chu Han - Y nghia hoac Tu vung - Phien am giup tang phan xa nhan dien mat chu.

8. Dich Cau (Sentence Puzzle)
   - Sap xep cac thanh phan cau de hoan thanh cau dich chinh xac theo ngu phap cua bai hoc.

9. Luyen Doc & Ngu Phap (Reading & Grammar View)
   - Bai doc ngan theo chu de co kem giai nghia tu moi va cau hoi doc hieu.
   - Tong hop kien thuc ngu phap kem vi du va tai lieu tham khao nhung truc tiep.

## Co Che Ghi Nho & Quan Ly Hoc Tap

- Thuat Toan SRS (Spaced Repetition System): Tu dong tinh toan chu ky lap lai cua tung tu dua tren lich su tra loi (Easy, Good, Hard, Again), toi uu hoa thoi gian on tap.
- Ngan Hang Cau Sai (Mistake Bank): Tu dong luu lai tat ca cac tu vung va Han tu tra loi sai trong cac phien kiem tra de nguoi hoc tap trung on tap bo khuyet.
- So Tay Ca Nhan (Custom Notebook): Cho phep tao danh sach hoc tap rieng biet, danh dau tu vung yeu thich hoac cac chu can chu y dac biet.
- Bang Thong Ke Ca Nhan (Personal Dashboard): Theo doi chi so streak hoc tap lien tuc, bieu do tien do hoan thanh theo tung cap do, va lich su hoat dong chi tiet.

## Huong Dan Cai Dat & Khoi Chay

### Yeu Cau He Thong
- Node.js: Phien ban 18.0.0 tro len
- Trinh quan ly goi: npm hoac yarn / pnpm

### Cac Buoc Thuc Hien

1. Clone ma nguon du an:
```bash
git clone https://github.com/anhtuan120605/LearnJPD.git
cd LearnJPD
```

2. Cai dat cac thu vien phu thuoc:
```bash
npm install
```

3. Thiet lap bien moi truong (tuy chon neu muon dung dong bo Supabase):
Tao tep `.env` tai thu muc goc cua du an voi noi dung:
```env
VITE_SUPABASE_URL=https://your-project-id.supabase.co
VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY=your-anon-key-here
```

4. Khoi chay may chu phat trien:
```bash
npm run dev
```
Truy cap trinh duyet tai: `http://localhost:5173`

5. Dong goi san pham:
```bash
npm run build
```
Kiem tra ban build truoc khi deploy:
```bash
npm run preview
```

## Cau Hinh Dong Bo Dam May (Supabase)

Ung dung co the hoat dong doc lap voi co che Offline LocalStorage. De bat tinh nang dong bo da thiet bi qua Supabase:

1. Tao mot du an moi tren [Supabase Console](https://supabase.com).
2. Mo giao dien **SQL Editor** trong Supabase Dashboard.
3. Sao chep noi dung tep [supabase/schema.sql](supabase/schema.sql) va chay lenh tao bang (`profiles`, `user_progress`, cac policies RLS).
4. Lay thong so `Project URL` va `anon public key` tai muc **Project Settings -> API** va dien vao tep `.env`.
5. Chi tiet cac buoc co the tham khao tai [supabase/README.md](supabase/README.md).
